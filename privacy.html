Data use and protection

Privacy policy.

Effective 17 November 2026. This policy describes the actual behaviour of Cxption Personal Drive Sync, a personally operated macOS automation using the Google Drive API through rclone. It applies to this specific private writing-sync utility, not to other Cxption services or websites.

1. Operator and contact

The utility is operated privately by the owner of the Google account that authorises it. For privacy, access, or deletion questions, contact posi2day@gmail.com. There is no public user registration or multi-tenant service.

2. Google user data accessed

To copy a selected set of writing documents, the rclone Google Drive connection requests Google Drive full-file access (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive). Google's permission prompt accordingly states that the app may view, edit, create, and delete Drive files. This permission is technically broader than the scheduled workflow's current purpose.

The scheduled commands are restricted by their configured source and destination paths to the owner's 04 Creative/Shared Writing folder and its Shared Writing Versions archive. They read file names, file content, sizes, timestamps, and other metadata necessary for transfer and version comparison. They may create or update files in the two selected Drive folders. Automated deletion is not enabled. The permissions technically allow wider access if the owner later changes the commands; any material change in use requires updating this policy.

3. What data is used for

  • Uploading eligible writing documents from the owner's MacBook Air to the owner's Google Drive.
  • Downloading copies of those documents to the owner's MacBook Pro.
  • Preserving previous versions when an eligible document is overwritten.
  • Checking transfer completion, recording limited diagnostic information, and notifying the owner of errors or authorisation expiry.

Data is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, training AI or machine-learning models, or unrelated purposes. The operator does not sell Google user data.

4. Storage, security and sharing

Transferred documents are stored on the owner's two Macs and in the owner's Google Drive account. OAuth access and refresh tokens and the private OAuth client configuration are stored locally in owner-restricted rclone configuration files; they are not published to this website or GitHub repository. Local audit files may include the sync mode, status, timestamps, errors, and file-related metadata. The public website contains no private Google user files or tokens.

Google processes files in its Drive service under the account holder's Google settings and policies. This desktop utility does not intentionally transmit Google user data to any additional external service or person. Its public informational pages are hosted by GitHub Pages; the pages do not request access to a visitor's Google account, use custom analytics, or serve personal Drive content.

The utility follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements: data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the user-visible file-sync function, is not transferred to data brokers or advertisers, and is not used to develop or train generalised AI models.

5. Retention and deletion

Shared documents and their saved previous versions remain until the account owner removes them from Google Drive or the local computers. The automation does not apply a fixed automatic retention or deletion period. Operational diagnostic records remain on the local machines until the owner deletes them. Disabling a scheduled job stops new transfers but does not automatically erase already-synchronised copies.

The owner may remove shared documents and version-history folders, delete locally stored copies and diagnostics, and revoke the OAuth permission in their Google Account connections. Revoking access prevents new Google Drive API requests until reauthorisation; it does not itself delete existing copies.

6. Access, corrections and policy updates

The utility is for the operator's own account; no other person is enrolled. The owner retains full control of their Drive files, local files, and Google OAuth authorisation. Questions or corrections can be sent to the contact above. This page will be updated if the utility's permissions, data destinations, sharing practices or functionality materially change; the effective date will change accordingly.
