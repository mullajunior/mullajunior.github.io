Use and limitations

Terms of use.

Effective 17 November 2026. These terms describe a private, non-commercial file-sync tool and its public informational pages. The tool is not being offered as a public service or subscription.

1. Personal use

Cxption Personal Drive Sync is operated for one person's desktop writing workflow. It uses the Google Drive API through rclone on the owner's Macs, with access approved by the Google account holder. There is no public sign-up, no hosted account system, and no service charge.

2. Scope of service

The configured schedule copies eligible writing documents from the owner's MacBook Air to a selected private Google Drive folder, then copies them down to the MacBook Pro. It retains previous versions of overwritten files, where configured, and does not perform scheduled deletions. It does not provide automatic two-way conflict resolution, a whole-computer backup, or guaranteed uninterrupted access.

3. Control and permissions

The owner remains responsible for selecting which documents are placed in the shared-writing folder, securing their device and Google account, reviewing any conflicts, and preserving independent backups if needed. Google OAuth access may be revoked through Google Account security settings at any time. Google's own account and Drive terms continue to govern Google-hosted files.

4. Availability and changes

This self-managed utility is provided as available for personal use. Synchronisation can be interrupted by a device being offline, changes to Google Drive or OAuth policies, credentials expiring or being revoked, file conflicts, or local software failures. The owner may modify or stop the tool and may revise this public explanation to reflect actual changes.

5. Privacy and contact

See the privacy policy for Google data permissions, storage, retention, and revocation. Contact posi2day@gmail.com with questions.
